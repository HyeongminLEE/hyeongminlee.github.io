Background

True Belief vs. False Belief

Same story, one sentence apart

TB

TRUE BELIEF

Noor is working as a barista at a busy coffee shop. Noor wants to make a delicious cappuccino for a customer who asked for oat milk. Noor grabs a milk pitcher and fills it with oat milk. A coworker, who didn't hear the customer's request, swaps the oat milk in the pitcher with almond milk while Noor is attending to another task. Noor sees her coworker swapping the milk.

FB

FALSE BELIEF

Noor is working as a barista at a busy coffee shop. Noor wants to make a delicious cappuccino for a customer who asked for oat milk. Noor grabs a milk pitcher and fills it with oat milk. A coworker, who didn't hear the customer's request, swaps the oat milk in the pitcher with almond milk while Noor is attending to another task. Noor does not see her coworker swapping the milk.

QUESTION

Does Noor believe the milk pitcher contains oat milk or almond milk?

BigToM item 1, full text (Forward Belief) · Noor's initial belief (oat milk) is not stated in the story
